Conversion consistency

Profile visitors should land on a fast, relevant page with a clear next action instead of a vague homepage or broken link.

Important rules

Do not manufacture eligibility.

A profile should represent a real eligible business. Misleading addresses, virtual offices, mailboxes, keyword-stuffed names, and fabricated locations create avoidable suspension risk.

Use the real business name
Do not add city names, services, or promotional phrases unless they are genuinely part of the business name.
Represent the real customer experience
Use accurate locations and service areas based on how the business actually operates.
Keep ownership controlled
The business owner should retain primary control of the profile, domain, analytics, and key accounts.
