Clear Lake, Oregon

The McKenzie’s headwaters lake — absurdly clear water, submerged trees, and the calmest “water morning” on the corridor before famous trailheads heat up.

Updated July 13, 2026

Best forQuiet mornings · shoreline loop · photos
Water tempCold year-round — plan accordingly
Stay nearbyClear Lake Resort area · upper corridor
Pair withSahalie/Koosah · Hwy 242 (seasonal)

What makes it different

Clear Lake formed in a volcanic basin and stays remarkably clear — you can see submerged forest from shore or boat. It’s less “Instagram chaos” than Blue Pool and a strong anchor if your trip is about water, not mileage.

Timing & crowds

Peak summer afternoons get busy at the day-use areas. Locals favor early loops, then head down-corridor for food or a second stop. Shoulder seasons are underrated for photography.

Lodging logic

If Clear Lake is your whole weekend, staying at the headwaters beats driving up from Eugene every morning. Compare upper-corridor options in where to stay — mid-corridor bases around Vida still work if you accept a longer first drive for lake sunrise.
