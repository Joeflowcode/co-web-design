Things to do on the McKenzie River

The hits are real — Blue Pool, Sahalie & Koosah, Clear Lake, Belknap — but timing and backups decide whether the weekend feels magical or jammed.

Updated July 13, 2026

The classic corridor stops

Tamolitch Blue Pool

Go early. Bring layers and shoes with grip. If the lot is circling, switch to Sahalie/Koosah or a quieter McKenzie River Trail segment instead of burning an hour hunting parking.

Sahalie & Koosah Falls

High reward, lower commitment. Perfect first morning stop when the group has mixed hiking appetites.

Clear Lake

Loop the shoreline, look for the submerged forest vibe, and treat it as a calmer water morning before the famous trailheads fill.

Belknap Hot Springs

The reset button after miles. Day-use soaks are popular; overnighting nearby simplifies the evening.

McKenzie River Trail (pick a segment)

You do not need all 26 miles. Segment hiking is the local move when Blue Pool is slammed — see quiet alternatives.

Spot guides

Deep dives with parking, timing, and stay tips: Blue Pool · Falls · Clear Lake · Belknap · Vida

Where you sleep changes the day

Upper stays favor Clear Lake mornings. Hot-springs stays favor soaks. Mid-corridor river lodging around Vida favors mixed itineraries — waterfall mornings without living at the trailhead. Compare bases in where to stay.

Quiet backups

  • McKenzie River Trail segments instead of Blue Pool at noon
  • Falls loop first, pool second (or skip the pool)
  • Seasonal Hwy 242 / Dee Wright when open for a lava-field add-on
