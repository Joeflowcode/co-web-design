Sahalie & Koosah Falls

Two powerhouse waterfalls close together — the corridor’s best “everyone in the group wins” morning stop when Blue Pool parking is already a mess.

Updated July 13, 2026

Best timeFirst thing Saturday or any weekday morning
EffortShort loop · family-friendly
Pair withClear Lake, Blue Pool, Belknap soak
AreaUpper McKenzie · near McKenzie Bridge

Why locals start here

Sahalie and Koosah deliver big waterfall energy without the all-day commitment of a major hike. The loop connects both falls through old-growth forest — enough trail feel for hikers, short enough for kids and grandparents.

On peak weekends this lot fills too, but turnover is often faster than Blue Pool because visits are shorter. It’s the default backup when Tamolitch is slammed.

Trail & safety

Stay on established paths and behind railings at viewpoints. Spray makes rocks slick. Winter ice is real — check conditions before you bring non-hikers.

Build a day around the falls

  • Classic loop: Falls → lunch toward Rainbow → optional Blue Pool if lots look sane
  • Calmer water day: Falls → Clear Lake shoreline
  • Soak day: Falls → Belknap Hot Springs → corridor dinner
