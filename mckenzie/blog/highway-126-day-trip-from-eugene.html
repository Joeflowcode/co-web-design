Highway 126 day trip from Eugene

Leave early, pick two stops max, eat before you’re hangry — the formula for a McKenzie day trip that doesn’t end in a sunset white-knuckle drive.

Published July 13, 2026

Sample one-day loop

  • 7:00am: Leave Eugene · coffee in town
  • 9:00am: Sahalie & Koosah Falls loop
  • 11:00am: Snacks / early lunch toward Rainbow
  • 12:30pm: Blue Pool only if parking looks reasonable
  • 4:00pm: Head back — optional quick stop at Vida river pullouts

When to stay overnight instead

If you’re adding Clear Lake and Blue Pool and dinner at Takoda’s, you’re building a two-day trip. Mid-corridor stays cut the return drive and let you hit falls at dawn. See where to stay.

Food planning

Corridor restaurants have limited hours — read restaurants before you assume a 7pm sit-down exists near the trailhead.
