Quiet alternatives when Blue Pool is packed

Full lot? Turn around with a plan B already loaded — circling for an hour is how good weekends go bad.

Published July 13, 2026

1. Sahalie & Koosah Falls first

Same corridor, shorter visit, faster parking turnover. Do the falls loop, eat, then drive past Blue Pool to see if lots opened up — often they didn’t.

2. McKenzie River Trail segment

You don’t need Tamolitch to hike the MRT. Pick a pullout segment for shade and river sound without the influencer queue. More in things to do.

3. Clear Lake morning

Swap a slammed midday pool for a Clear Lake shoreline loop — especially good for families and photographers.

4. Soak instead of hike

If the group is fried, pivot to Belknap and call it a win. Not every McKenzie day needs a famous trailhead checkbox.

5. Come back tomorrow at dawn

Mid-corridor stays around Vida make a 6:30am Blue Pool attempt realistic without a pre-dawn Eugene drive.
