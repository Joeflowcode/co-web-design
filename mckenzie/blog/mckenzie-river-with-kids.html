McKenzie River with kids

You don’t need to hike 26 miles to call it a success. Pick two wins and quit while everyone’s still smiling.

Published July 13, 2026

Best kid-friendly stops

  • Sahalie & Koosah Falls: Big payoff, short loop, hold hands at viewpoints.
  • Clear Lake: Shoreline time without a steep climb.
  • Belknap Hot Springs: Pools when legs are tired — confirm family policies before you go.

What to save for a return trip

Blue Pool can work with older kids who handle longer trails and crowds, but it’s a poor first stop for a mixed-age group on a packed Saturday. Do falls first; reassess Blue Pool only if parking looks sane.

Packing & timing

  • Snacks before you leave the car — corridor markets are sparse.
  • Layers: spray at falls, sun at lake, cold at trailheads.
  • Start early; nap in the car on the drive down if you must.

Where to stay with kids

Mid-corridor river stays around Vida shorten Friday drives and give river downtime without campground setup. Some riverfront properties include complimentary rafting/float experiences with the stay — useful when you want river time without rigging your own gear.
