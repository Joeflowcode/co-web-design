McKenzie River weekend itinerary

A simple two-day shape that works from Eugene or Bend without stacking every famous stop into one sweaty afternoon.

Published July 12, 2026 · Updated July 13, 2026

Friday night

Get on the corridor early enough to settle. Mid-corridor bases around Vida shorten the Friday drive from Eugene and still keep Saturday waterfall mornings realistic. Upper stays make more sense if Clear Lake is your whole focus.

Saturday

  • Early: Sahalie & Koosah Falls while lots are calm
  • Late morning: Blue Pool if parking looks reasonable — otherwise a McKenzie River Trail segment
  • Afternoon: snacks from a corridor market, optional Clear Lake add-on
  • Evening: Takoda’s or Obsidian Grill, then river downtime at your stay

Sunday

  • Belknap soak if legs are cooked
  • Or a short shoreline / trail segment and an earlier drive home
  • If Hwy 242 is open, Dee Wright is a scenic add-on toward Sisters

Where this itinerary works best

If your group wants waterfall mornings and quieter evenings, mid-corridor river lodging tends to fit better than sleeping beside the busiest trailheads. Compare Clear Lake, Belknap, campgrounds, and riverfront options like Vida View Resort in the where to stay guide — that Vida base is also a fit when someone in the group wants a complimentary rafting/float with the stay, or you’re scouting a McKenzie River wedding venue on the same trip. Pick lodging by tomorrow’s first stop, not by whichever listing looks fanciest.
