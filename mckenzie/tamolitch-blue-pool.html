Tamolitch Blue Pool

The iconic McKenzie River hike — worth it when you time it right, frustrating when you arrive at peak Saturday with a full lot and circling cars.

Updated July 13, 2026

Best timeWeekday mornings or before 9am on weekends
TrailMcKenzie River Trail · moderate out-and-back
ParkingTrailhead lots fill fast May–October
BackupSahalie/Koosah or MRT segments

What to expect

Blue Pool is a collapsed lava tube section where the McKenzie runs impossibly clear and turquoise. The hike itself is the draw — not a swim spot (cold, fast water, and rules change; treat it as look-don’t-touch unless posted otherwise).

Social media made this stop famous. On summer Saturdays the trailhead can feel like a festival parking lot. Locals treat Blue Pool as a morning stop or skip it when lots are already turning cars away.

Parking & access

Most visitors use the Tamolitch / McKenzie River Trail access off Highway 126. Arrive early, carpool if you can, and have a backup plan before you commit to circling for 45 minutes.

Local move

If the lot is full, drive to Sahalie & Koosah Falls first — high reward, easier parking rhythm — then reassess Blue Pool on the way back if energy and crowds allow.

Best season & weather

  • Spring–fall: Busiest; go early.
  • Winter: Fewer people, more mud/ice on trail — check Willamette NF conditions.
  • After rain: Trail can be slick; wear shoes with grip.

Where to stay for Blue Pool mornings

Upper-corridor stays near Clear Lake or Belknap shorten the drive. Mid-corridor bases around Vida work if you’re okay leaving before breakfast on hike day — useful for mixed groups who want river evenings without camping at the trailhead.
