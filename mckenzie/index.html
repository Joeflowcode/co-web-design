Weekend shape

A simple McKenzie loop

One pattern that works for most first-timers without overpacking the day.

Morning

Upper corridor

Sahalie & Koosah Falls early, or Clear Lake if you want a quieter shoreline loop before parking turns over.

Midday

Tamolitch / Blue Pool

Go for Blue Pool when you can handle a busier trail — or swap to a McKenzie River Trail segment if the lot is already circling.

Afternoon / evening

Soak + corridor dinner

Belknap soak if legs are cooked, then Takoda’s or Obsidian Grill. Mid-corridor stays make the drive home feel shorter.

FAQ

Questions travelers ask first

Where should we stay on the McKenzie?

Match the stay to your mornings. Upper-corridor for Clear Lake and falls. Belknap if soaking is the point. Mid-corridor around Vida if you want waterfall access without sleeping next to the busiest lots — some riverfront stays there also include a complimentary rafting/float and can host weddings on-site. Compare options in the where to stay guide.

Is Blue Pool always worth it?

Yes if you go early. On peak afternoons, Sahalie/Koosah or a quieter McKenzie River Trail segment often feels better.

Where do people eat?

Plan on Takoda’s, Obsidian Grill, and corridor markets. Hours flex with seasons — see the restaurants guide.
