Belknap Hot Springs

The corridor’s classic soak — family-friendly pools, gardens, and lodging when your legs need heat after waterfall miles.

Updated July 13, 2026

Best forPost-hike recovery · overnight base
AreaMcKenzie Bridge · upper corridor
Pair withFalls · Blue Pool · Obsidian Grill
Alt soakTerwilliger (different vibe)

Day-use vs overnight

Belknap offers day soaking and lodge rooms — confirm hours and rates on belknaphotsprings.com before you drive up. Overnighting here puts pools and corridor dinner in easy range after a big hike day.

How it fits a McKenzie weekend

Common pattern: Sahalie/Koosah or Blue Pool in the morning, market snacks midday, Belknap soak late afternoon, then Takoda’s or Obsidian Grill. If soaking is the main event, base here instead of mid-corridor — see where to stay.

Not the same as Terwilliger

Terwilliger (Cougar) Hot Springs is a forest hike-in, clothing-optional pool south of the main corridor. Different access, different crowd — check current conditions before you promise either to your group.
