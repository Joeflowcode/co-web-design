FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

How much does a website cost?

Packages run from $1,499 for a basic site to $25,000+ for premium builds, with most growing businesses landing in the $7,500–12,000 Professional range. Every quote is fixed and itemized before we start — no hidden fees, ever.

How long does it take to build?

Starter sites take 6–8 weeks; Professional and Premium builds run 8–12. We start development in a staging environment as soon as your homepage is approved, and you'll see progress at every step.

Why do I need SEO?

A beautiful site nobody finds is a brochure in a locked drawer. SEO is how customers searching "near me" actually reach you — more qualified visitors, more leads, no ad spend required.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes — redesigns are a specialty. We modernize the design, improve speed and UX, and migrate your content carefully so you keep the search authority you've already earned.

Do you offer SEO without a new website?

Yes. Standalone SEO starts at $500/month and covers keyword research, technical audits, on-page optimization, and monthly reporting on your existing site.

Can AI chatbots really recommend my business?

They already recommend someone — the question is whether it's you. We add the structured data, FAQ formatting, and authority signals that make AI systems cite your business when customers ask.

Are you actually local?

Yes — based in Bend, serving Redmond, Sisters, La Pine, and all of Central Oregon. We know this market because we operate in it.

Do you guarantee #1 rankings?

No legitimate agency can. What we do guarantee: a proven process, transparent monthly reporting, and the track record above. Our clients consistently see significant ranking and traffic gains.
