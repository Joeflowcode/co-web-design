FAQ

Questions before you book.

Straight answers so you can plan with confidence.

What if it rains?

Pacific Northwest weather happens. If rain or high wind makes the night unsafe or unwatchable, we reschedule with you at no penalty. Light drizzle can sometimes continue under covered spaces — we’ll decide together the day of.

How long does setup take?

Most Basic Cinema setups take about 45–75 minutes. Full Night Out (with bouncy house) usually needs a bit longer. We’ll arrive early enough that your guests never wait on gear.

Can we really watch any movie?

Yes — bring the title you want on a streaming account, USB, or disc. We connect playback to our projector and speakers. For public or ticketed events, ask us about licensing so everything stays above board.

What kind of power do we need?

A standard outdoor outlet is enough for most backyard setups. Campgrounds and larger community nights may need a dedicated circuit — mention your venue when you request a quote and we’ll confirm.

Do you travel outside Lyons–Bend?

Our core corridor is Lyons, Detroit Lake, Salem, Sisters, and Bend. Nearby towns are often easy to add. Farther trips may include a travel fee — just ask.

Is there a deposit?

A modest deposit holds your date once you approve the quote. The balance is due before showtime. We’ll spell out payment details in your confirmation.
