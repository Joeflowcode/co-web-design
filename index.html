Our process

Done right in four steps.

A beautiful floor starts with preparation. We diamond-grind every surface before a single drop of epoxy goes down — that's what separates a 3-year floor from a 15-year floor.

Step 01

Free on-site estimate

We measure your space, inspect the concrete, discuss colors, and give you a clear written quote — no pressure.

Step 02

Grind & prep

Diamond grinding opens the concrete pores. We repair cracks, fill joints, and apply primer for maximum adhesion.

Step 03

Base coat & flake

Colored epoxy base coat goes down, then we broadcast vinyl flakes to full rejection for even coverage and texture.

Step 04

Clear top coat

Polyaspartic clear coat locks everything in — UV-stable, chemical-resistant, and ready for traffic in 24 hours.

FAQ

Common questions.

How long does installation take?

Most single-car and two-car garages are completed in 1–2 days. Day one is prep and base coat with flake. Day two is top coat and cure. You can usually walk on it within 24 hours and park on it within 72 hours.

How long will my epoxy floor last?

With proper prep and a quality multi-coat system, expect 15–20+ years of service in a residential garage. The flake texture also hides minor wear better than solid-color epoxy.

Can you coat over old epoxy or paint?

Sometimes, but only if the existing coating is well-bonded. We'll test it during your estimate. In most cases, we recommend grinding down to bare concrete for the best long-term result.

Is it slippery when wet?

Full flake broadcast creates a textured surface that's significantly more slip-resistant than smooth epoxy or bare concrete. It's a great choice for Oregon's wet climate.

What if my concrete has cracks or moisture?

We repair cracks and fill expansion joints before coating. If moisture is a concern, we use a moisture-barrier primer. We'll flag any issues during the free estimate — no surprises on install day.

Do you offer warranties?

Yes — every installation includes a written workmanship warranty. Material warranties come from our epoxy manufacturers. We'll explain coverage details in your quote.
