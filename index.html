FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

Is the website really free?

Yes — there's no upfront design fee. Your custom website is included when you join any monthly marketing package. After 12 paid months, the website files are yours to keep forever. If you cancel before 12 months, the free-build benefit doesn't apply.

What's included in the monthly fee?

Hosting, website updates, SEO work, Google Business posts, reporting, and ongoing marketing support — everything listed in your package. The monthly fee covers active service, not just hosting.

What happens if I cancel early?

All plans require a 12-month commitment. If you cancel before completing 12 paid months, you either pay a website release fee to keep the site files or forfeit the free-build benefit. Hosting, edits, and marketing support stop when your subscription ends.

Which package should I choose?

Starter Visibility ($300/mo) is great for small businesses getting online. Growth Engine ($500/mo) is our most popular — best for contractors, dentists, HVAC, and local service businesses. Local Domination ($700/mo) is for high-ticket businesses where one new customer pays for the plan.

How long does it take to launch?

Most sites launch within 3–4 weeks of signing up. We start building as soon as you approve the plan, and monthly marketing work begins at launch.

Do you use templates?

Every site looks custom to your brand. Behind the scenes we use proven layouts and AI-assisted workflows — that's how we deliver a free website and ongoing marketing at a monthly price that makes sense.

Can you help with my existing website?

If you already have a site, we'll review it during your free audit and recommend whether a rebuild or refresh makes more sense. Many clients start fresh to get the full free-website benefit.

Are you actually local?

Yes — based in Bend, serving Redmond, Sisters, La Pine, and all of Central Oregon. We know this market because we operate in it.
