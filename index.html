FAQ

Before you book.

What if it rains?

If rain or wind kills the night, we reschedule at no penalty. Covered spaces sometimes keep a light drizzle going — we’ll call it together that day.

How long is setup?

Basic Cinema is usually 45–75 minutes. Full Night Out with a bouncy house needs a little more. We arrive early so guests never wait on gear.

Any movie we want?

Yes — streaming account, USB, or disc. For public or ticketed events, ask us about licensing.

No outlet at the venue?

No problem — we bring a quiet generator so the show still runs at parks, fields, and campgrounds. Add it on the quote form or mention it when you call.

Power requirements?

A standard outdoor outlet covers most yards. No outlet? Add our generator. Campgrounds and big community nights — tell us the venue and we’ll size power correctly.

Outside Portland metro or Lyons–Bend?

We cover Hillsboro, Lake Oswego, West Linn, Beaverton, and neighboring suburbs, plus the Valley and Cascades corridor. Nearby towns are often easy; farther trips may include travel — just ask.

Deposit?

A modest deposit holds the date after you approve the quote. Balance before showtime.
