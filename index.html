FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

Is the website really free?

Yes — no upfront design fee. Your custom website is included with any monthly package. After 12 paid months, the files are yours forever. Cancel before 12 months and the free-build benefit doesn't apply.

What's included in the monthly fee?

Hosting, website updates, SEO work, Google Business posts, reporting, and ongoing marketing support — everything in your package. The monthly fee covers active service, not just hosting.

What happens if I cancel early?

All plans require a 12-month commitment. Cancel early and you either pay a website release fee or forfeit the free-build benefit. Hosting, edits, and marketing support stop when your subscription ends.

Which package should I choose?

Starter ($300/mo) for small businesses getting online. Growth ($500/mo) is most popular — contractors, dentists, HVAC. Local Domination ($700/mo) for high-ticket businesses where one customer pays for the plan.

How long does it take to launch?

Most sites launch within 3–4 weeks. Monthly marketing work begins at launch.

Do you use templates?

Every site looks custom to your brand. Behind the scenes we use proven layouts and efficient workflows — that's how we deliver a free website at a monthly price that makes sense.

Are you actually local?

Yes — based in Bend, serving Redmond, Sisters, La Pine, and all of Central Oregon.
