# SEO and launch checklist

## Domain
- Buy or select the final domain.
- Keep the domain registered in the business owner's account.
- Update canonical URLs, Open Graph URLs, schema URLs, sitemap URLs, and robots.txt.
- Force one preferred HTTPS hostname.

## Contact and conversion
- Connect the audit form to Formspree, Tally, HubSpot, GoHighLevel, or another tested endpoint.
- Add the real business email and phone number.
- Test every form, email, and click-to-call action on desktop and mobile.
- Add a thank-you page and conversion event.

## Trust
- Add the owner's real name and photo.
- Add three real portfolio examples with screenshots and project details.
- Add only testimonials and results that can be verified and published with permission.
- Add the real service agreement terms, privacy policy, and cancellation terms.

## Search setup
- Verify the domain in Google Search Console.
- Submit /sitemap.xml.
- Inspect the homepage, service pages, and location pages.
- Install analytics and track forms, calls, and booked consultations.
- Validate structured data and mobile performance.

## Content growth
- Add Redmond, Sisters, Eugene, Portland, and Albany pages only when the business genuinely serves those markets.
- Add industry pages for the best target customers, such as contractors, roadside assistance, junk removal, party rentals, restaurants, and vacation-rental services.
- Avoid mass-produced or nearly identical city pages.

## Priority order
1. Domain and contact form.
2. Real portfolio proof.
3. Search Console and analytics.
4. Industry pages.
5. Additional genuine service-area pages.
