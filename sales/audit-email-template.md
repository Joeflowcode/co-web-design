## Tips

- **Personalize line 1.** Mention a real review, a service they offer, or something from their site.
- **One competitor name** makes the audit feel real — don't bash them, just state the gap.
- **Send Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10am** local time for best open rates.
- **Attach or link?** Paste the audit in the email for short audits. For detailed ones, use a Google Doc or PDF export of `website-audit-template.md`.
- **Record a Loom** (2–3 min) walking through their Google results — often doubles reply rates.
